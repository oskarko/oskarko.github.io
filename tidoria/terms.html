Tidoria es un juego de puzle: agrupas productos en estanterías hasta dejar el tablero ordenado. Los niveles se generan automáticamente y se comprueba que tengan solución antes de mostrártelos. 3. What Tidoria is

Tidoria is a puzzle game: you group goods on shelves until the board is tidy. Levels are generated automatically and checked to have a solution before you are shown them. It is not a game of chance, there is no betting, there are no real-world prizes and there are no loot boxes: every purchase states exactly what it delivers.

4. In-app purchases

The game is free and complete. Optionally, you can buy:

  • Coin packs (consumable purchase): they pay out a fixed, stated number of coins. Coins are also earned by playing, they pay for hints, extra shelves and cosmetic themes, they have no value outside the game and they cannot be exchanged for money.
  • Remove Ads (one-off purchase): removes the ads between levels and the optional videos.
  • All Themes (one-off purchase): unlocks the cosmetic themes, which change how the game looks and never how hard it is.

There are no subscriptions. Payment is processed by Apple through the App Store and delivery is verified with RevenueCat. One-off purchases are recovered with "Restore Purchases" in the game's shop. Refunds are handled by Apple according to their policies; we cannot grant them.

5. Advertising
