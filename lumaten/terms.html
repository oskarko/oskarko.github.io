3. Qué es LumaTen

LumaTen es un puzle de números. El tablero es una rejilla de fichas numeradas: tocas dos y la pareja se limpia si los números son iguales o si suman diez. Las dos fichas tienen que estar seguidas en la misma fila, columna o diagonal, contando solo las casillas ya vacías entre ellas, y el final de una fila conecta con el principio de la siguiente. Cuando limpias una fila completa, desaparece. El nivel se gana vaciando el tablero, y cuando no queda ninguna pareja puedes añadir una fila de la reserva del propio nivel. Las estrellas —tres, dos o una— dependen solo de cuántas filas de reserva hayas gastado. Hay 600 niveles de campaña, un reto diario, un evento semanal, un modo contrarreloj de 90 segundos y un modo sin fin. No es un juego de azar, no hay apuestas, no hay premios reales y no hay cajas de botín: cada compra dice exactamente qué entrega.

3. What LumaTen is

LumaTen is a numbers puzzle. The board is a grid of numbered tiles: you tap two and the pair clears if the numbers are equal or if they add up to ten. The two tiles have to be consecutive along the same row, column or diagonal, counting only the already-empty squares between them, and the end of one row connects to the start of the next. Clear a whole row and it disappears. A level is won by emptying the board, and when no pair is left you can add a row from the level's own reserve. Stars — three, two or one — depend only on how many reserve rows you spent. There are 600 campaign levels, a daily challenge, a weekly event, a 90-second timed mode and an endless mode. It is not a game of chance, there is no betting, there are no real-world prizes and there are no loot boxes: every purchase states exactly what it delivers.

4. In-app purchases

The game is free and complete: all 600 levels, the daily challenge, the weekly event, the timed mode and endless can be played in full without spending a penny. Optionally, you can buy:

  • Coin packs (consumable purchase): they pay out a fixed, stated number of coins — 600, 1,800 or 6,000. Coins are also earned by clearing levels and they pay for the four boosters — undo (25), hint (30), shuffle (40) and prism (60) — as well as for three of the five colour themes (120, 180 and 240). Retrying a level is free and always will be. They have no value outside the game and they cannot be exchanged for money.
  • Remove Ads (one-off purchase): removes the ad between levels and hands over directly the extra Prism that is otherwise obtained by watching a video.
  • All Themes (one-off purchase): unlocks the three themes coins can buy — Sunset Pop, Ocean Glow and Violet Night — plus Prism Club, which is not on sale for coins, and any added later. They change the background and the six tile colours, and never the difficulty or the solution of a level.

There are no subscriptions. Payment is processed by Apple through the App Store with StoreKit; keeping track of what you have unlocked is done for us by RevenueCat. One-off purchases are recovered with "Restore Purchases" in the shop or the game's settings. Refunds are handled by Apple according to their policies; we cannot grant them.

Buying does not buy stars. A level's reserve rows are a fixed number that cannot be extended with money or with coins, and stars are worked out from how many you spent: no purchase raises a level's score.
