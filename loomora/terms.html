3. Qué es Loomora

Loomora es un nonograma —también llamado picross— presentado como punto de cruz: deduces de las pistas de cada fila y columna qué casillas se bordan hasta completar el patrón. Los patrones se generan automáticamente y se comprueba que tengan solución única alcanzable por lógica antes de mostrártelos. No es un juego de azar, no hay apuestas, no hay premios reales y no hay cajas de botín: cada compra dice exactamente qué entrega.

3. What Loomora is

Loomora is a nonogram — also called picross — presented as cross stitch: you deduce from the clues on each row and column which squares are stitched until the pattern is complete. Patterns are generated automatically and checked to have a unique solution reachable by logic before you are shown them. It is not a game of chance, there is no betting, there are no real-world prizes and there are no loot boxes: every purchase states exactly what it delivers.

4. In-app purchases

The game is free and complete. Optionally, you can buy:

  • Coin packs (consumable purchase): they pay out a fixed, stated number of coins — 500, 1,500 or 5,000. Coins are also earned by playing, they pay for hints and cosmetic themes, they have no value outside the game and they cannot be exchanged for money.
  • Remove Ads (one-off purchase): removes the ads between patterns and the optional videos.
  • All Themes (one-off purchase): unlocks the cosmetic themes, which change the linen, the thread and the accent colour, and never the difficulty.

There are no subscriptions. Payment is processed by Apple through the App Store with StoreKit, with no intermediary. One-off purchases are recovered with "Restore Purchases" in the game's shop. Refunds are handled by Apple according to their policies; we cannot grant them.
