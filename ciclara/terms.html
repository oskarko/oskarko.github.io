5. Sobre las predicciones y los patrones

Las predicciones de la siguiente regla y de la fase se calculan con la mediana de tus últimos ciclos, y su precisión depende por completo de la exactitud y la constancia de tu registro: unos pocos ciclos anotados dan una predicción pobre, y así te lo dice la app.

Los patrones que Ciclara encuentra entre tus fases y tus datos de Apple Health son correlaciones descriptivas sobre tu propio historial, con umbrales mínimos de datos antes de afirmar nada, y nunca una relación de causa y efecto. Están redactados para describir lo que registraste, no lo que tienes. La fórmula y las fuentes de cada número están dentro de la app, en la sección de metodología, y esa sección es gratuita para siempre: nadie debería pagar para saber cómo se ha calculado un número sobre su cuerpo.

3. Purpose and health warning

Ciclara is a personal menstrual cycle logging and analysis tool: you record your periods and your symptoms, and the app computes patterns and predictions from that. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, does not treat, does not recommend treatments, and does not replace the judgement of a healthcare professional. If a symptom worries you, talk to your doctor, not to this app.

4. It is not a contraceptive method

This deserves a heading of its own. The fertile window and the estimated ovulation Ciclara shows are statistics, not observed biology: they are computed from the dates you log, and ovulation varies from cycle to cycle even in very regular ones. Ciclara must not be used to avoid pregnancy, as a substitute for a contraceptive method, or to confirm or rule out a pregnancy. If you are trying for or avoiding a pregnancy, or think you may be pregnant, talk to your doctor.

5. About the predictions and the patterns

The next-period and phase predictions are computed from the median of your recent cycles, and their accuracy depends entirely on how accurate and how consistent your log is: a handful of logged cycles makes for a poor prediction, and the app tells you so.

The patterns Ciclara finds between your phases and your Apple Health data are descriptive correlations over your own history, with minimum data thresholds before anything is claimed, and never a cause-and-effect relationship. They are worded to describe what you recorded, not what you have. The formula and sources behind every number are inside the app, in the methodology section, and that section is free forever: nobody should have to pay to find out how a number about their own body was reached.
