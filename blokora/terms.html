3. Qué es Blokora

Blokora es un puzle de bloques con gravedad: sueltas piezas sobre un tablero, completas filas y lo que queda encima se desploma. Tiene una campaña de 150 niveles con objetivos y movimientos contados, un modo sin fin y un reto diario con clasificación. No es un juego de azar, no hay apuestas, no hay premios reales y no hay cajas de botín: cada compra dice exactamente qué entrega.

4. Compras dentro de la app

El juego es gratuito y completo. De forma opcional puedes comprar:

  • Packs de monedas (compra consumible): abonan un número fijo y anunciado de monedas —500, 1.500 o 5.000—. Las monedas también se ganan jugando, sirven para el martillo, para cambiar las piezas de la mano y para los temas de color, no tienen valor fuera del juego y no son canjeables por dinero.
  • Quitar anuncios (compra única): elimina los anuncios entre partidas y hace que seguir jugando tras un «sin movimientos» no requiera ver ningún vídeo.
  • Pack de temas (compra única): desbloquea las cuatro paletas premium y las que se añadan más adelante. Cambian los colores del tablero y de las piezas, y nunca la dificultad.

No hay suscripciones. El pago lo procesa Apple a través del App Store con StoreKit; la gestión de qué has desbloqueado la realiza RevenueCat por nuestra cuenta. 3. What Blokora is

Blokora is a block puzzle with gravity: you drop pieces onto a board, complete rows, and whatever sits above them collapses. It has a 150-level campaign with objectives and counted moves, an endless mode and a daily challenge with a leaderboard. It is not a game of chance, there is no betting, there are no real-world prizes and there are no loot boxes: every purchase states exactly what it delivers.

4. In-app purchases

The game is free and complete. Optionally, you can buy:

  • Coin packs (consumable purchase): they pay out a fixed, stated number of coins — 500, 1,500 or 5,000. Coins are also earned by playing, they pay for the hammer, for swapping the pieces in your hand and for colour themes, they have no value outside the game and they cannot be exchanged for money.
  • Remove Ads (one-off purchase): removes the ads between games and means carrying on after a game over needs no video at all.
  • Theme pack (one-off purchase): unlocks the four premium palettes and any added later. They change the colours of the board and the blocks, and never the difficulty.

There are no subscriptions. Payment is processed by Apple through the App Store with StoreKit; keeping track of what you have unlocked is done for us by RevenueCat. One-off purchases are recovered with "Restore Purchases" in the game's settings. Refunds are handled by Apple according to their policies; we cannot grant them.

5. Advertising

The game is funded by advertising (Google AdMob): ads between games and optional videos you can watch to earn coins or to continue a lost game. Watching a video is always voluntary and never the only way forward. If you buy "Remove Ads", they are gone.
