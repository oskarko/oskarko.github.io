3. Qué es Cromora

Cromora es un puzle de deslizar pigmentos. Arrastras un borrón de pintura y este resbala hasta que algo lo detiene: un muro, otro borrón o el borde de la mesa. Solo el rojo, el amarillo y el azul empiezan sobre la mesa; al empujar dos primarios uno contra otro se fusionan en naranja, verde o violeta. Cada nivel se gana vaciando la mesa: todo borrón tiene que salir por una boca que pida su color y aún tenga hueco en el pedido. Cada nivel tiene un número de movimientos objetivo y se puntúa con tres, dos, una o ninguna estrella según cuántos hayas gastado. Hay un reto diario, común para todo el mundo ese día, que se clasifica por menos movimientos. No es un juego de azar, no hay apuestas, no hay premios reales y no hay cajas de botín: cada compra dice exactamente qué entrega.

Las monedas también se ganan jugando y sirven para deshacer un movimiento (10), para una pista (25) y para cinco movimientos más cuando te has quedado sin ellos (40), además de para los temas de color. Reintentar un nivel es gratis y siempre lo será. No tienen valor fuera del juego y no son canjeables por dinero.

Cambian los colores de la mesa y de los pigmentos, y nunca la dificultad ni la solución de un nivel.

3. What Cromora is

Cromora is a sliding-pigment puzzle. You drag a blob of paint and it slides until something stops it: a wall, another blob or the edge of the table. Only red, yellow and blue start on the table; push two primaries into each other and they fuse into orange, green or violet. Each level is won by emptying the table: every blob has to leave through a mouth that wants its colour and still has room in its order. Each level has a target move count and is scored three, two, one or no stars depending on how many you spend. There is a daily challenge, the same one for everybody that day, ranked by fewest moves. It is not a game of chance, there is no betting, there are no real-world prizes and there are no loot boxes: every purchase states exactly what it delivers.

4. In-app purchases

The game is free and complete: any level can be finished without spending a penny. Optionally, you can buy:

  • Coin packs (consumable purchase): they pay out a fixed, stated number of coins — 600, 1,800 or 6,000. Coins are also earned by playing and they pay for undoing a move (10), for a hint (25) and for five more moves when you have run out (40), as well as for colour themes. Retrying a level is free and always will be. They have no value outside the game and they cannot be exchanged for money.
  • Remove Ads (one-off purchase): removes the ads between levels and makes the five extra moves — otherwise paid for with coins or by watching a video — free.
  • Theme pack (one-off purchase): unlocks the four themes coins can buy — Pastel, Midnight, Forest and Candy — plus the Ink theme, which is not on sale for coins, and any added later. They change the colours of the table and the pigments, and never the difficulty or the solution of a level.

There are no subscriptions. Payment is processed by Apple through the App Store with StoreKit; keeping track of what you have unlocked is done for us by RevenueCat. One-off purchases are recovered with "Restore Purchases" in the shop or the game's settings. Refunds are handled by Apple according to their policies; we cannot grant them.

Buying moves does not buy stars. Finish a level on bought moves and it counts as cleared, but stars are worked out from the total moves spent: paying does not raise your score.
