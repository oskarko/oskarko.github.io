La puntuación que muestra la app no es una probabilidad de éxito: mide el peso de los motivos que has seleccionado y lo completos que están los datos introducidos. 3. What Multa Cero is and is not

Multa Cero is an informational tool: it reads the data on a Spanish traffic-fine notice, points out which grounds of appeal might fit that data, works out the deadline for filing and drafts a document arguing the grounds you select.

It is not legal advice and it creates no attorney-client relationship. We are not a law firm, we do not review your file and we do not file anything on your behalf. The app cannot and does not guarantee that your appeal will succeed, nor that the deadlines, grounds or text-recognition results are correct or complete for your particular case. Reviewing the document, checking the dates and deciding whether to file it are your responsibility, and for cases with a significant amount or consequences (licence points, repeat offences, proceedings already at the enforcement stage) we recommend consulting a professional.

The score the app shows is not a probability of winning: it measures the weight of the grounds you selected and how complete the data you entered is. Some grounds assert facts only you can confirm (that the signage was not visible, that your papers were valid…); those grounds are never selected for you, and by including them you state that they are true and that you can substantiate them.
